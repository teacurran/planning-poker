We're thrilled to announce the official launch of **Planning Poker**, a real-time collaborative estimation tool built from the ground up for modern Agile teams.

## Why We Built Planning Poker

In today's world of distributed and remote work, Agile teams need tools that enable seamless collaboration regardless of location. Traditional planning poker sessions often rely on physical cards or cumbersome tools that create friction in the estimation process.

We set out to solve this problem by building a platform that combines:

- **Zero friction onboarding** - Start estimating in seconds with no registration required
- **Real-time synchronization** - Sub-second latency for votes and reveals
- **Powerful analytics** - Track team velocity and estimation accuracy over time
- **Enterprise readiness** - SSO integration, audit logging, and organization management

## Key Features

### Instant Anonymous Play

Get started immediately with our Free tier. No credit card required, no complicated setup—just create a room and share the link with your team. Perfect for trying out the platform or conducting quick estimation sessions.

### Real-time Collaboration

Our WebSocket-based architecture ensures that votes and reveals happen instantly across all participants. Whether your team is across the office or across the world, everyone stays perfectly synchronized.

### Flexible Pricing Tiers

We offer four subscription tiers to match teams of all sizes:

- **Free**: Perfect for trying out Planning Poker with basic functionality
- **Pro ($10/month)**: Ad-free experience with advanced reports and export capabilities
- **Pro Plus ($30/month)**: Enhanced privacy controls and invite-only rooms
- **Enterprise**: Custom pricing with SSO, audit logging, and dedicated support

### Advanced Analytics

Pro and Enterprise tiers unlock powerful analytics features:

- Round-level estimation detail
- User consistency metrics
- Team velocity tracking
- Export to CSV, JSON, and PDF

### Enterprise Security

For organizations with compliance requirements, our Enterprise tier includes:

- SSO integration (OIDC and SAML2)
- Comprehensive audit logging
- Organization-restricted rooms
- Unlimited session history

## Technology Stack

Planning Poker is built with modern, production-ready technologies:

- **Frontend**: React with TypeScript for a responsive, type-safe UI
- **Backend**: Quarkus (Java) for high-performance API services
- **Real-time**: WebSockets for sub-second synchronization
- **Infrastructure**: Kubernetes for scalable, resilient deployment
- **Database**: PostgreSQL for reliable data storage
- **Monitoring**: Prometheus and Grafana for observability

## What's Next

We're just getting started. Here's what's coming in the next few months:
